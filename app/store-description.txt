Rabbit Hole turns Wikipedia into something you can read on your glasses.

Say a topic. Rabbit Hole finds the article, rewrites it as a short, plain-language breakdown, and plays it one large word at a time in the middle of the display, with the paragraph alongside. Linked terms are marked: open one and you are a level deeper, then come back to where you were.

- Speak a topic, or type it on your phone
- Hold the ring to ask a question about what you are reading
- Pictures from the article beside the section they belong to
- Reopen topics you have read before from your history
- Change the reading speed from the menu

You need your own API keys to use it: an Anthropic key for the breakdowns, and an ElevenLabs key if you want to speak topics. Add them under "Keys and access" on the phone-side page. They are kept on your phone and sent only with your own requests.
